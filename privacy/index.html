sagitogo — Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Who this is for

sagitogo is made for children to use, with a parent or guardian setting it up. This policy is written so a parent can read the whole thing in a few minutes and know exactly what happens to their family’s information. A shorter, plainer version is in the app itself — Parent Dashboard → Privacy & support.

Who we are

sagitogo is published by QNL LTD, 92 Cloonmore Avenue, Orpington, Kent, BR6 9LQ, company number 09869549 — the same company that publishes Sagito. QNL LTD is the data controller. Questions about this policy, or about data held about you, go to support@sagitogo.app. QNL LTD has not appointed a Data Protection Officer and is not required to.

Because the app sends nothing to us, we hold no personal data about you or your child and have nothing to look up, export or delete on your behalf. Everything is on your device, under your control.

The short version

Everything sagitogo knows about your family stays on your device. There is no sagitogo account, no sign-in, and no sagitogo server at all — so there is nowhere for your child’s name, progress or anything they’ve typed to go, and nobody to send it to.

The only other parties sagitogo ever talks to are Apple or Google, when you choose to buy the one-off unlock, and only to process that purchase.

What is kept, and where

All of it is stored only on this device, in the app’s own private storage area (not a shared folder, not visible to other apps):

  • Family members. For each person added: a name or nickname (a first name or any nickname will do, and it is the only text entered), a chosen avatar appearance (not a photo), and when they were added.
  • Progress. Questions answered, times tables and spelling/vocabulary practised, streaks, space gems, robot cards found, trail-find items found, which companion a child has chosen, their highest score (and how many questions it took) at each Mission level, and whether they have switched the read-aloud speaker on — kept per family member.
  • The parent PIN and its recovery word. Kept in your phone’s own secure credential storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android) — not in the app’s ordinary storage, and not readable by sagitogo itself once set, only checked against.
  • A sibling PIN, if a family member sets one — a lock between brothers and sisters sharing a device, not a secret kept from a parent.

Never kept, anywhere: your location, what anyone looks like in real life, how old a child is, your name, your email address, or anything typed into a message to someone else. There is no chat inside sagitogo.

The camera and gift codes

The camera is used for exactly one thing: reading a QR code a friend shows you on their own screen, to receive a robot card or an item they’ve sent. It works the way a shop’s barcode scanner does. No photo is ever taken, and nothing from the camera is ever saved.

A sent code carries only: a one-off id, which robot card or item it is, whether a card is an upgraded copy, and when it was made. It does not carry either child’s name or anything else about who sent it or who it’s for.

A sent robot or item is a gift, not a request — once the code is made, it can’t be taken back or declined, only opened or left unopened. If nobody opens it within 7 days, it’s gone.

Hearing a question

A speaker switch on each question screen reads the question (or, on spelling, the word) aloud. It starts switched off for every family member, and the phone remembers each member’s choice. It uses the voice built into the phone, only once that member has switched it on, and only for the question or word shown on screen. The app does not record anything, never uses the microphone, and asks for no permission. What is spoken is not sent to us or to anyone by this app. (Some Android phones use a voice that downloads from the phone maker; that is the phone’s own setting and not something this app controls.) Nothing depends on it: the question is always on screen.

Practice reminders

A parent can switch on one reminder a day from the parent area. It is off until they do, and the phone asks permission only at that moment. The reminder is created and shown on the phone itself: it does not use push notifications, no token is registered with Apple, Google or us, and nothing is sent to any server. It is the same on-device kind of notification as an alarm clock.

The words never mention a child’s name, a score, a streak or anything they have or haven’t done. If nobody opens the app for a few days, the reminders stop by themselves. Switching it off, turning notifications off in the phone’s Settings, or choosing “Delete everything on this device” removes every pending reminder.

Money

The one-off unlock is paid for and processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play — never by sagitogo directly. No card number, billing address or other payment detail ever reaches the app. Checking what’s already been bought, and completing a purchase, are the only times sagitogo’s network connection is used, and nothing about your child is part of that conversation.

Deleting it

Delete everything on this device, in Parent Dashboard, erases every family member, all of their progress, the record of which gift codes have already been opened, the unlock flag, and the parent PIN and recovery word themselves.

What it cannot reach: a robot card already sent to or received from another family, and your phone’s own backups — if your phone backs itself up to iCloud or a Google account, a copy of sagitogo’s data may exist there until that backup is itself deleted or ages out.

Uninstalling the app does the same thing on most phones, with one difference some devices handle differently: a small number of Android configurations may not clear the Keychain-equivalent PIN automatically on uninstall. Using “Delete everything on this device” before uninstalling is the surest way to leave nothing behind.

Who can see what sagitogo sends or receives

Nobody, besides Apple or Google for the one purchase-related check above. sagitogo has no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking of any kind built into it.

Children’s privacy

sagitogo is built for use by children, with a parent or guardian choosing to install it and setting the parent PIN. No account or sign-in is ever asked of a child, no personal detail beyond a name or nickname and a drawn avatar is ever collected, and nothing is ever shared outside the device without a parent’s own action.

Your rights, and your child’s

Under UK GDPR you and your child have rights to see, correct, delete and object to the use of personal data. Because everything is on your device, these are things you do yourself rather than ask us for — all of them are in Parent Dashboard, behind the PIN:

  • See it — Progress reports shows each family member’s level, streak and accuracy by subject.
  • Correct it — a family member can be renamed at any time, and their avatar changed.
  • Delete some of it — Reset progress clears one family member’s own points, cards and quiz history.
  • Delete a family member — Family members lets you remove one, together with everything they earned.
  • Delete all of it — Delete everything on this device erases every family member, all progress, your PIN and your recovery word.
  • Take it elsewhere — not currently built.

Because we hold nothing ourselves, there is no request you can make to us that would produce data we’re sitting on — but if any of the above doesn’t work the way this page says, tell us at support@sagitogo.app and we’ll fix it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If what sagitogo does with data ever changes, this page changes with it, and the version inside a given build of the app reflects what that build actually does.
