Free resources

Things to print and use at home, whether or not you ever download Sagito Go. No sign-up, no email address.

Printable times tables

Three A4 pages: the 12 × 12 times tables square, every table from 1 to 12 with the answers, and a blank practice sheet to fill in and check.

Download the times tables (PDF, A4, about 250 KB)

Times tables: gaps and missing numbers

Once the tables are familiar: a square with gaps to fill in, thirty-six missing-number questions such as 7 × ? = 56 that make you work backwards, and all the answers.

Download gaps and missing numbers (PDF, A4, about 250 KB)

KS2 spelling lists

The statutory spelling lists for Years 3 and 4 and Years 5 and 6, with tick boxes and a look, say, cover, write, check practice sheet. Word lists from the national curriculum for England, © Crown copyright, under the Open Government Licence.

Download the KS2 spelling lists (PDF, A4, about 200 KB)

KS2 spelling tracker

One page for each list, with a row for every word and three tick boxes: practised, right a week later, right a month later. Fill it in as the words become secure.

Download the spelling tracker (PDF, A4, about 270 KB)

A word a day

One ambitious word for every school day of the year, 195 in all, each with how to say it, a plain meaning, an example sentence and the ordinary word it replaces. The last page is a notebook to fill in. They are the same words as the Word of the Day card on the Sagito Go home screen.

Download A word a day (PDF, A4, about 800 KB)
