Space gems, real facts, forty robots to find

sagitogo is daily recall practice for ages 4 to 12 — times tables, spelling, vocabulary and reasoning — set aboard a spacecraft a child fills with robot companions as they go.

Four things to practise

  • Spelling & vocabulary — words to learn and remember
  • Times tables — quick recall, not working out
  • Reasoning — codes, patterns and sequences
  • Mission mode — a mixed round against the clock

No account, no adverts

There is no sagitogo server. Everything a family does stays on the device it’s installed on, and nothing is ever sold to a child. Read the privacy policy.
