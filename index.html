Five minutes a day. Spelling, times tables and reasoning, at exactly the right level.

Sagitogo is short, quick practice that children actually finish. Every answer moves a mission forward, and every so often a robot is found and added to the collection.

Coming soon to the App Store. Available in the UK.

What it covers

Spelling

From common words to the tricky ones, with wrong answers that look like real slips.

Times tables

Recall, division, missing numbers and beyond. Quick recall, not working out.

Vocabulary

Words that mean the same, words that mean the opposite, and meaning in context.

Reasoning

Patterns, codes and sequences, from the simple to the genuinely tricky.

It finds the right level

Sagitogo watches how each answer goes and adjusts, aiming for practice that is challenging but not discouraging. There is no level to choose, and no long test to sit first.

Five stations, forty robots

Each station is a place to explore. Keep practising, and robot companions turn up, each with a trading card to keep and a real fact to learn. Children can pick a favourite to travel with them, and swap cards with family.

Practice earns space gems, which children spend in the spacecraft shop on kit for their collection. Nothing is ever sold to a child.

Every explorer deserves a companion

Children pick a robot to travel with, and it cheers them on as they practise. The adventure is better shared, too: cards can be swapped and gifted with family, as a request the other person can decline rather than something that happens to them.
